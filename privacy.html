# ClipperBot – Privacy Policy **Last updated: November 21, 2026** ClipperBot is designed to help users manage their own TikTok video publishing workflow. **Information accessed** If the user authorizes an official TikTok connection, the application may access account information and content permissions required for its enabled features. **Purpose** Information is used only to provide the features requested by the user. **Data protection** ClipperBot is designed to minimize data collection and protect access credentials. Personal information is not sold or rented to third parties. **Third-party services** TikTok operates independently and its own privacy policy applies to information processed by its services. **User control** Users may revoke TikTok access through their account settings. **Contact** For privacy questions, contact the application administrator through the website where this policy is published.
